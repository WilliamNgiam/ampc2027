Annual Meeting of the ASMP

Australasian Mathematical Psychology Conference2027

The annual meeting of the Australian Society for Mathematical Psychology (ASMP) will be hosted by Adelaide University in 2027. This conference brings together mathematical psychologists and cognitive scientists from around Australia and beyond to discuss quantitative and computational approaches to psychological phenomena.

Workshop

Flexible implementation of Bayesian hierarchical cognitive models with the EMC2 package

Andrew Heathcote, Niek Stevenson, Sarah Kemp

EMC2 is an R package for Bayesian hierarchical analysis of cognitive models of choice. It supports flexible and efficient estimation of commonly used models, such as the diffusion decision model and race models, as well as reinforcement-learning evidence accumulation models. We will open this workshop with a roadmap of EMC2's full functionality, and an introductory material on the core workflow. This is followed by short demonstrations of its key features. Code and example data are provided for every topic, ensuring newcomers to cognitive modelling can follow the whole workflow, whilst more experienced users can adapt the provided code for their own purposes.

Participants are assumed to have experience with R and an understanding of cognitive modelling. Basic understanding of EMC2 is useful; we will provide an introductory tutorial prior to the workshop.

Code of Conduct

All attendees, speakers, sponsors and volunteers at our conference are required to agree with the following code of conduct.

Our conference is dedicated to providing a harassment-free conference experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, religion (or lack thereof), or technology choices.

We do not tolerate harassment of conference participants in any form. Sexual language and imagery is not appropriate for any conference venue, including talks, workshops, parties, BlueSky/Twitter and other online media.

If you have any concerns during your time at the conference, please contact one of the conference organisers.
