The Australasian Mathematical Psychology Conference (AMPC) is the annual meeting of the Australian Society for Mathematical Psychology (ASMP).

This year, the conference will be hosted on February 10-12 by Adelaide University. This conference brings together mathematical psychologists from around Australia and beyond to discuss their latest research.
